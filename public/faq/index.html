FAQ

Answer the questions customers usually ask before ordering.

Here are the most common questions about ordering, personalisation, custom work, and delivery options.

Can I order without creating an account?

Yes. Guest checkout is supported, while returning customers can also create an account.

Can I request a custom print?

Yes. Use the Custom Print page or order notes if your request does not fit a standard product page.

What kinds of files or references can I provide?

STL files, 3MF files, logos, photos, and simple written descriptions are all helpful starting points for a custom request.

How do I pay?

Orders are set up for PayPal checkout, and draft orders can also be saved while final details are being confirmed.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. The checkout flow supports pickup or delivery selection.
