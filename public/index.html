Frequently asked questions

How do I pay for my order?

Choose your items, add delivery details and continue to secure checkout when it is available for your order.

Where are Kiavik pieces made?

Kiavik pieces are made to order in Melbourne, Australia, after you choose your options.

Can customers request something custom?

Yes. The enquiry form on this page submits through Netlify Forms, and product cards can also offer custom text, colour and size selection before checkout.

Do customers need an account?

No. Guest checkout is supported, and accounts are only there for people who want order history.
